Who we are

Built by two engineers who were tired of machines that didn't get serviced.

SP Solutions started in Chennai in 2019, when two colleagues who'd spent years maintaining packaging lines for other companies decided to build the support system they wished they'd had — machinery paired with people who actually pick up the phone.

Today we design, supply, install, and service shrink wrap, flow wrap, and end-of-line systems for food, pharma, and FMCG manufacturers across Tamil Nadu, with spares stocked so a worn part never becomes a lost shift.
